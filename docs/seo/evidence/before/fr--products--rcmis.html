Aperçu

RCMIS

RCMIS is the financial backbone of OPES-connected facilities. It receives billable acts automatically from OPES EMR, OPES Lab, PHARMIS, and RADIS — building a complete, accurate patient invoice without any manual data entry by the billing team. Every consultation, lab test, dispensed medicine, and imaging study is captured.

Insurance claims, CNPS submissions, and mutual fund reimbursement requests are generated automatically from the same billing data and tracked through to payment. Revenue dashboards give finance directors real-time visibility on collections, outstanding claims, and payer mix.

À qui est destiné RCMIS ?

  • Hospital finance and billing departments
  • Private clinic owners and administrators
  • Multi-facility groups managing consolidated revenue reporting
  • Insurance claims processing teams
  • CNPS-affiliated healthcare providers

Problèmes résolus par RCMIS

1

Billable acts missed due to paper-based billing — Every clinical act from every OPES system posts automatically — zero billing leakage.

2

Insurance claim rejections from incomplete data — Claims are built from validated clinical data with all required fields pre-populated.

3

No visibility on outstanding payments — Real-time aging reports show every unpaid invoice by payer, age, and amount.

4

Manual CNPS submission errors — CNPS-format claims generated automatically from the billing data with correct tariff codes.
