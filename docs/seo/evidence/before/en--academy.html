Certification tracks

Six tracks, one goal: digital excellence

Digital Clinician
OPES Certified Clinical User
Doctors, nurses, midwives
Mastery of electronic patient records, digital prescriptions, triage, and consultation workflows in OPES EMR.
EMR & patient records Prescriptions & orders Triage & admissions Consultation management
Lab Technician
OPES Certified Lab Technician
Biologists, technicians, lab staff
Managing test requests, validating results, quality control, and EMR integration via OPES Lab.
Request management Result validation Quality control EMR integration
Pharmacy Manager
OPES Certified Pharmacy Manager
Pharmacists, dispensers, store managers
Pharmaceutical stock management, dispensing, expiry alerts, and drug interaction management in PHARMIS.
Stock management Dispensing Drug interactions Pharmacy reports
Health Data Administrator
OPES Certified Health Data Administrator
IT officers, data managers, CIOs
System administration, user management, backups, HMIS reporting, and DHIS2 exports for health partners.
System administration HMIS reporting DHIS2 export User management
Clinical Intelligence Analyst
OPES Certified Clinical Intelligence Analyst
Epidemiologists, public health officers, researchers
Advanced use of analytics dashboards, epidemiological surveillance, and interpretation of clinical performance indicators.
Advanced dashboards Epidemiological indicators Performance reporting AI decision support
OPES System Engineer
OPES Certified System Engineer
IT engineers, integrators, CIOs
Deployment, advanced configuration, HL7 FHIR integrations, open API administration, and OPES infrastructure maintenance.
Deployment & infrastructure FHIR integrations API administration Security & hardening
