About OPES Health Systems

Building Africa's digital health infrastructure

Founded in Cameroon and built for Africa, OPES Health Systems SARL develops interoperable healthcare solutions that connect providers, patients, laboratories, pharmacies, insurers, and public health programs through a unified digital ecosystem.

Our Mission

To eliminate fragmented healthcare information, improve continuity of care, and empower healthcare professionals across Africa with modern, interoperable digital tools that improve clinical outcomes and operational efficiency — starting from Cameroon and the CEMAC region.

Our Vision

A CEMAC region where every patient has a universal Health ID and their complete medical record follows them seamlessly — from first contact at a village health post through specialist care at a teaching hospital — all connected through the OPES Care interoperability layer.

Our Story

Born from Cameroon's healthcare realities

OPES Health Systems grew out of direct experience with the challenges of healthcare delivery in Cameroon: duplicate patient records across facilities, lost prescription histories, manual laboratory logbooks, and revenue leakage from disconnected billing systems.

We set out to build what we couldn't find — a comprehensive, bilingual, Africa-first healthcare ecosystem. Not a port of a Western EMR with French toggled on, but software designed from the ground up for the Cameroonian clinical workflow, CNPS billing requirements, and HL7 FHIR interoperability standards.

Based in Bonamousadi, Douala, our team combines clinical knowledge, software engineering, and deep understanding of the CEMAC health sector. OPES is aligned with the Cameroon Ministry of Health 2026–2030 national digitalization plan and supports the Universal Health Coverage mandate.

Today, the OPES Health Platform comprises more than twenty specialized solutions — from Electronic Medical Records and Hospital Information Systems to Laboratory and Pharmacy platforms, specialty clinical applications, and a Health Information Exchange layer — serving facilities ranging from independent clinics to large hospital networks and national health programs.

Interoperability Layer

At the core: OPES Care

OPES Care is a patient-centered interoperability framework that enables secure information exchange across healthcare organizations. It supports longitudinal patient records, continuity of care, and seamless communication between every system in the ecosystem — ensuring the right information reaches the right provider at the right time.

OPES Health OS

One platform, six product families

OPES Health OS is our unified health operating system. It groups six product families — from patient records to clinical intelligence — all interoperable, bilingual, and designed to work together or independently.

OPES Clinic
Primary & outpatient care digitalisation
EMR, OPD workflows, triage, prescriptions, and patient identity for clinics, private practices, and health centres.
OPES Hospital
Full hospital information system
End-to-end hospital operations: inpatient management, nursing, lab, pharmacy, radiology, maternity, and billing.
OPES Specialty Suite
Departmental & specialist modules
Dedicated modules for radiology (RADIS), laboratory (OPES Lab), pharmacy (PHARMIS), maternity, oncology, and renal care.
OPES Care
Patient identity & care coordination
OPESCare universal health ID, longitudinal patient record, mobile patient portal, and cross-facility care continuity.
OPES Clinical Intelligence
Analytics, BI & public health reporting
Real-time dashboards, epidemiological surveillance, HMIS reporting, and AI-assisted clinical decision support.
OPES Academy
Workforce development & certification
Role-based digital health training, system certification programmes, and continuing professional development for health workers.
Governance

Corporate governance and operating model

OPES Health Systems operates under a rigorous governance model that aligns our product decisions with real clinical needs, regulatory compliance, and the sustainability of our clients. Our governance architecture includes technical, clinical, and ethics committees that oversee every evolution of the platform.

Board of Directors
Strategic oversight of the company, setting long-term direction and investment priorities.
Technical & Product Committee
Governance of the technology roadmap, architecture validation, dependency management, and technical risk.
Clinical Governance Committee
Review of clinical modules by African health practitioners to ensure the medical relevance of every feature.
Compliance & Ethics Committee
Supervision of OHADA compliance, Cameroonian health data law, GDPR-alignment, and clinical AI ethics.
Risk Management
Identification, assessment, and mitigation of operational, financial, and service continuity risks for our clients.
Product Review Cycle
Quarterly product performance reviews integrating customer feedback, incidents, and regulatory developments.
