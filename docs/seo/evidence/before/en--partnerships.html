Partnerships

Partner with OPES Health Systems

We work with healthcare facilities, government bodies, NGOs, hardware vendors, and insurance providers who share our vision for a connected, digital Cameroon health system.

Healthcare Facility Partners

Pilot facilities, reference sites, and early adopters who help shape OPES systems and benefit from preferential pricing and direct support from our Douala team.

  • Preferential implementation pricing
  • Co-development of features for your context
  • Case study and testimonial visibility
  • Direct line to OPES engineering team

Government & Ministry Partners

National health programmes, district health teams, and Ministry of Health departments deploying population-scale digital health infrastructure.

  • MoH 2026–2030 alignment documentation
  • HMIS integration and reporting
  • UHC tracking and population dashboards
  • Multi-site deployment support

NGO & International Partners

WHO, UNICEF, MSF, and international health organisations running programmes in Cameroon and the CEMAC region.

  • Donor reporting and programme dashboards
  • Multi-language support beyond EN/FR on request
  • Offline-first deployment for remote areas
  • Open data export in FHIR format

Technology Partners

Hardware vendors, medical device manufacturers, and IT service companies who want to bundle OPES into their healthcare offerings.

  • Reseller agreements and revenue sharing
  • API integration and OEM licensing
  • Joint marketing in the CEMAC market
  • Technical certification programme

Insurance & CNPS Partners

Health insurance providers, mutuals, and CNPS looking to integrate digital claims verification and patient identity through OPESCare.

  • Real-time eligibility verification
  • HL7 FHIR claims data
  • Fraud reduction through patient Health ID
  • CNPS billing module integration

Academic & Research Partners

Universities, medical schools, and research institutions using OPES for clinical education, research data, and public health studies.

  • Anonymised de-identified data access
  • Research module integration
  • Clinical training environment
  • Publication co-authorship opportunities
