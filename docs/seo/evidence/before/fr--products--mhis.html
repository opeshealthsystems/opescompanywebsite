Aperçu

MHIS

MHIS provides structured clinical records for mental health facilities — psychiatric inpatient units, community mental health centres, addiction treatment programmes, and outpatient psychiatry clinics. It implements privacy-first data architecture, ensuring mental health records are accessible only to authorised mental health professionals.

MHIS includes validated assessment tools (PHQ-9, GAD-7, AUDIT, CAGE, PANSS, YMRS) embedded in the clinical workflow, alongside structured therapy session notes, medication management (including depot injection scheduling), and crisis care protocols.

À qui est destiné MHIS ?

  • Psychiatric inpatient units
  • Community mental health centres
  • Addiction treatment and rehabilitation programmes
  • Outpatient psychiatry and psychology clinics
  • NGO mental health programmes

Problèmes résolus par MHIS

1

Mental health records not appropriately privacy-protected — MHIS records are isolated from general OPES EMR access — only authorised MH professionals can view.

2

Validated assessment tools not used consistently — PHQ-9, GAD-7, PANSS, and other validated tools embedded in every assessment workflow.

3

Depot injection schedules missed — Automatic alerts for due depot injections and long-acting medication administrations.

Modules

Tout en un seul système

Chaque module s'intègre nativement — commencez avec ce dont vous avez besoin aujourd'hui, développez au fur et à mesure de votre croissance.

Psychiatric Assessment

Structured psychiatric assessment with MSE, risk assessment, and validated rating scales.

  • Mental State Examination (MSE)
  • PHQ-9 · GAD-7 · PANSS · YMRS
  • Suicide & risk assessment tools
  • ICD-10 psychiatric diagnosis coding

Therapy & Session Notes

Structured notes for individual therapy, group sessions, and family therapy.

  • Session type templates
  • Progress note SOAP format
  • Goal tracking & outcomes
  • Group session attendance

Psychiatric Medication

Medication management with depot scheduling, side effect monitoring, and pharmacy integration.

  • Depot injection schedule
  • Side effect rating scales (UKU)
  • Controlled substance registry
  • Integration with PHARMIS

Avantages clés

Pourquoi les établissements choisissent MHIS

Privacy-protected records

Mental health records isolated from general clinical access — only authorised MH professionals.

Validated assessment tools

PHQ-9, GAD-7, PANSS and more embedded — no paper forms, consistent application.

Depot injection alerts

Never miss a depot injection — automatic alerts for every due administration.

Pharmacy integration

Psychiatric medications prescribed directly to PHARMIS including controlled substances.
