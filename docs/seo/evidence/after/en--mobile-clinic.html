What we're building

Healthcare for forgotten zones

Too many rural communities live more than 50 km from the nearest health facility. We are changing that.

Mobile Clinics

Fully equipped medical vehicles with basic diagnostics, essential medicines, and OPES connectivity for consultations and patient follow-up.

Community Health Centres

Semi-permanent structures built in isolated villages, staffed by a resident nurse, basic equipment, and telemedicine access.

Rural Dispensaries

Small care posts stocked with essential medicines, managed by community health workers trained through OPES Academy.

Digital Connectivity & OPES

Each site is equipped with satellite or GSM connectivity and OPES software for patient tracking, vaccination reminders, and epidemic alerts.

Health Worker Training

Training local community health workers through OPES Academy — basic diagnostics, first aid, maternal and child health.

Maternal & Child Health

Priority focus on antenatal consultations, assisted deliveries, and child vaccination in high maternal mortality zones.

How to support

Every contribution counts

Whether you are an individual wellwisher, a company, an NGO, or an international organisation — there is a way to contribute to this mission.

Individual donation

Any amount is welcome. A donation of XAF 50,000 covers medical supplies for a mobile clinic for one week.

Corporate sponsorship

Fully sponsor a mobile clinic (~XAF 8M) and your logo appears on the clinic and in all our reports.

NGO / institutional partnership

Join us as an operational partner to co-design and co-fund sites in your area of operation.

In-kind donations

Medicines, medical equipment, vehicles, generators — contact us to discuss in-kind donations.
