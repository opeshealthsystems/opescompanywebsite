Overview

UHC IS

UHC IS is built for national and regional health authorities implementing Universal Health Coverage programmes. It manages beneficiary registration, coverage entitlements, facility claims verification, and population-level health reporting — providing governments, NGOs, and international donors with real-time visibility into programme effectiveness.

Integrated with OPESCare Health ID, every UHC beneficiary is linked to their complete health record. Facilities report UHC services directly into the system; claims are validated automatically against registered entitlements before payment processing.

Who is UHC IS for?

  • National and regional Ministries of Health
  • Government UHC programme administrators
  • International health funding bodies (WHO, World Bank, GAVI)
  • NGOs managing community health programmes
  • Social insurance funds (CNPS and equivalent)

Problems UHC IS solves

1

No real-time visibility on UHC coverage uptake — Live dashboards show service utilisation, coverage gaps, and beneficiary activation rates.

2

Ghost beneficiaries and fraudulent claims — OPESCare Health ID verification eliminates duplicate beneficiary registrations and fake claims.

3

Manual facility reporting delays — Facilities submit UHC service data directly into the system — no paper forms, no postal delays.

4

No geographic coverage mapping — Heat maps show which districts are under-served and where programme expansion is needed.

  • Payment status tracking
