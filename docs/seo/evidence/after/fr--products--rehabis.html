Aperçu

REHABIS

REHABIS provides a complete rehabilitation information system for physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and multidisciplinary rehabilitation teams. It includes structured functional assessment tools (Barthel Index, FIM, Berg Balance Scale), goal-setting with outcome tracking, session records for individual and group therapy, and integration with ORTHOIS for prosthetics and orthotics coordination.

REHABIS supports rehabilitation pathways for stroke, traumatic brain injury, orthopaedic surgery, spinal cord injury, and chronic musculoskeletal conditions — tracking patient progress from acute rehabilitation through community reintegration.

À qui est destiné REHABIS ?

  • Hospital physiotherapy departments
  • Rehabilitation centres and long-term care facilities
  • Stroke and neurological rehabilitation units
  • Orthopaedic post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Community rehabilitation programmes

Problèmes résolus par REHABIS

1

Rehabilitation goals not set or tracked — SMART goal-setting with regular review — progress tracked using validated outcome measures.

2

MDT communication fragmented — Shared MDT notes and goal board visible to all team members — physio, OT, speech, nursing, and doctor.

Modules

Tout en un seul système

Chaque module s'intègre nativement — commencez avec ce dont vous avez besoin aujourd'hui, développez au fur et à mesure de votre croissance.

Functional Assessment

Validated functional outcome measures at admission, discharge, and follow-up.

  • Barthel Index · FIM
  • Berg Balance Scale
  • Oxford Muscle Strength grading
  • Pain scale (NRS/VAS)

Goal Setting & Therapy

SMART rehabilitation goals with session records and outcome tracking.

  • SMART goal templates
  • Session record by discipline
  • Group therapy attendance
  • Home exercise programme printing

MDT Coordination

Shared team goal board and communication notes for multidisciplinary rehabilitation.

  • Shared MDT goal board
  • Cross-discipline notes
  • MDT meeting records
  • Discharge summary generation

Avantages clés

Pourquoi les établissements choisissent REHABIS
