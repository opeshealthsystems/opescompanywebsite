Why OPES

Built for the realities of African healthcare

Unlike generic health software adapted for Africa, OPES was designed from scratch for Cameroonian clinical workflows, bilingual interfaces, and CEMAC regulatory requirements.

Fully bilingual (EN/FR)
Every screen, report, and document in both French and English. No translation patches — built-in from day one.
HL7 FHIR R4 native
Industry-standard interoperability built in. Any OPES system can talk to any other — or to your existing equipment.
Works offline
Low-connectivity mode keeps your facility running during outages. Data syncs automatically when the connection returns.
MoH 2026–2030 aligned
Designed alongside the Cameroon Ministry of Health national digitalization roadmap for UHC and HMIS compliance.
Cloud & on-premise
Deploy on our secure cloud or install on your own servers. Both options get full support and updates.
Local support in Douala
