Security and project assurance

Requirements depend on the product, country, hosting arrangement and your facility’s agreement.

This website is not evidence of certification, government endorsement or regulatory compliance. Request the documents applicable to your project.

Data protection reference

Cameroon’s Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 concerns personal data protection. Consult the official text and have your project’s obligations reviewed by legal counsel. Referencing the law does not establish OPES compliance. Official Presidency text

Evidence to request

Ask for product scope, available test results, responsibilities and any applicable independent assessments. Check the dates, scope and issuer of any certificate.

Privacy and health data

Establish processing purposes, access permissions, hosting location, retention, backups and incident handling in the project agreements. Do not submit patient information through public forms.

Hosting and continuity

Confirm storage locations, protection measures, recovery objectives and support arrangements. Any availability guarantee must be recorded in a signed agreement.

Interoperability

A standard mentioned in a product overview does not prove that an interface is available or tested. Confirm versions, connectors and testing for your facility’s actual systems.
