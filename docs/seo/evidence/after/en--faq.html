Questions and answers about OPES

Direct answers to help plan your healthcare software project.

What is OPES Health Systems?

OPES Health Systems develops healthcare software in Cameroon. Its catalogue covers hospital, clinic, laboratory, pharmacy and specialist workflows. Contact the team to confirm product scope and availability for your facility.

How can I see a product demonstration?

Use the Request a Demo page to describe your facility and the products you want to discuss. The team will contact you to arrange the demonstration. The public website does not provide shared demo passwords.

How much does an OPES deployment cost?

Request a written project quote identifying products, facilities, users, hosting, implementation, training and support. Any website pricing should be confirmed in the final quote and agreement.

Which languages are available?

The corporate website provides English and French navigation and information. Some product descriptions and course materials are available in English only. Confirm the required languages for each product and training package before contracting.

Can OPES connect to our existing software?

Integration depends on the product, interface and version involved. Share the systems and data flows you need, then agree the connector scope and acceptance tests. A reference to HL7 FHIR or another standard does not establish compatibility with every system.

What support and uptime are included?

Support hours, response times, availability targets, maintenance and exclusions must be specified in your signed service agreement. The website does not establish a universal uptime guarantee or a standard 24-hour support promise.

Is OPES certified or government endorsed?

Do not treat this website or a product listing as evidence of certification or government endorsement. Ask for relevant assessment or approval documents and verify their issuer, dates and scope for your project.

Can I send patient records through the contact form?

No. Use public forms only for business enquiries and do not include patient records or sensitive clinical information. Agree any clinical data exchange, privacy responsibilities and secure transfer method as part of the project.

Does the software replace a clinician?

Product and website information is not medical advice. Clinical use, safety checks, training and professional responsibilities must be assessed by the facility before deployment.

How do I sign in or reset my password?

Use the single Sign in page for your OPES account. Choose Forgot password and enter the email associated with your account. Account permissions determine which portal you can access after sign-in.
